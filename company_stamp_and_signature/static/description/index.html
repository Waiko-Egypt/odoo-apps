📌 About This Module
Company Stamp & Signature is a complete solution for printing your company's official stamp and authorized signature on business documents inside Odoo 19.

Configure everything — image upload, size, position, opacity, rotation, watermark mode, and expiry date — directly from Settings or the Company form. Each employee can carry their own personal signature with a customizable Authorized Signatory Label. All changes are tracked in the chatter for a full audit trail, and access is restricted to the Stamp & Signature Manager group.

📖 How to Use
1

Install the Module

Go to Apps, search for Company Stamp, and click Install. The module requires: sale, account, stock, purchase, hr.

2

Add Yourself to the Stamp Manager Group

Go to Settings → Users & Companies → Users, open your user, and under Company Stamp & Signature, set the role to Stamp & Signature Manager.

⚠ Without this group you will not see the Stamp & Signature tab or Settings section.
3

Upload Stamp & Signature — Quick Way (Wizard)

Go to Settings → Stamp & Signature and click “Upload Stamp & Signature”. A dialog opens with two tabs — Company Stamp and Company Signature. Upload your images, set the display options, choose which documents to print on, then click Save.

4

Upload via Company Form — Full Settings

Go to Settings → Companies → [Your Company] and open the Stamp & Signature tab for the complete configuration, including:

  • Stamp image, width, height, opacity, position, rotation
  • Watermark mode toggle
  • Stamp & Signature expiry dates with auto-hide warning
  • Print on: Quotations, Invoices, Delivery Notes, Purchase Orders
  • Signature image and display settings
  • Authorized Signatory Label (company-level)
5

Access Settings from the Dedicated Menu

Go to Settings → Stamp & Signature in the left menu. Click the entry to open the Upload Wizard directly — configure all stamp and signature options from a single dialog.

💡 The wizard validates that an image is uploaded before allowing print options to be saved.
6

Add a Personal Signature per Employee

Open Employees → [Employee] → Personal Signature tab. Enable Use Personal Signature, upload the image, set size/position/opacity, and optionally enter a custom Signatory Label.

💡 The employee’s personal signature takes priority over the company signature on relevant documents.
7

Allow Employees to Manage Their Own Signature

Employees can also set their personal signature from My Profile → My Signature (top-right menu → Preferences). They do not need the Stamp Manager group for this.

8

Print a Document and Verify

Open a Quotation, Invoice, Delivery Note, or Purchase Order and click Print. The stamp and/or signature will appear on the PDF automatically if enabled and not expired.

💡 Use Watermark Mode for a background stamp effect on the full page.
🖨️ Configuration Reference

Stamp Settings

  • Stamp Image — PNG or JPG, recommended transparent background
  • Width / Height (px) — default 150 × 150 px
  • Opacity (0.1 – 1.0) — 1.0 = fully opaque, 0.3 = semi-transparent
  • Position — Left, Center, or Right alignment on the document
  • Rotation (°) — rotate 0–360°; e.g. 45° for a diagonal stamp
  • Watermark Mode — prints stamp as a centered full-page background image
  • Expiry Date — stamp auto-hides after this date; leave empty for no expiry
  • Print Toggles — enable independently for Quotations, Invoices, Delivery Notes, Purchase Orders

Signature Settings

  • Signature Image — PNG or JPG, recommended transparent background
  • Width / Height (px) — default 150 × 80 px
  • Opacity (0.1 – 1.0) — default 1.0
  • Position — Left, Center, or Right
  • Rotation (°) — rotate 0–360°
  • Expiry Date — signature auto-hides after this date; leave empty for no expiry
  • Show Signatory Label — displays a text label below the signature
  • Label Text — e.g. “Authorized Signatory”, “General Manager”

🔧 Technical Notes
🐍 _get_document_signature() returns a dict: {'image', 'width', 'height', 'opacity', 'position', 'rotation', 'label'}.
👁️ View syntax uses Python-expression invisible="..." — the old attrs attribute was removed in Odoo 17+.
🎨 All QWeb templates use Bootstrap 5 alignment classes: text-start, text-center, text-end.
🖨️ Report engine: wkhtmltopdf — compatible with Odoo 19's standard PDF pipeline. Binary images use 'data:image/png;base64,{}'.format(...).
🔌 Delivery slip inheritance applied via post_init_hook for maximum compatibility across Odoo 19 builds.
🔄 Fully compatible with Odoo 19 Community and Enterprise editions.
