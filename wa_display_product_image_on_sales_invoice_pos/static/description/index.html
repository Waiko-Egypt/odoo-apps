Display Product Images on Quotations, Sale Orders & Invoices

Enhance your business documents by automatically rendering each product's image inside Quotations, Sale Orders, and Customer Invoices. With a single toggle in company settings, every report line shows a clear product photo next to the product name — making documents instantly recognisable to your customers.
Key Features
 
 
01

Native Product Images

Uses each product's standard Odoo image directly in printable business documents.
 
02

Quotation & Order PDF

Product images appear automatically in the sale quotation / order PDF report table.
 
03

Invoice PDF Report

Product images are also rendered inside customer invoice PDF reports.
 
04

Odoo 16 Settings

Uses the native Odoo 16 settings layout and stores report options per company.
 
05

One-Click Setup

Enable or disable image display from the general Settings page and configure its report dimensions.
 
06

Custom Image Size

Control the exact width and height (in pixels) of product images in reports to match your brand.
 
07

Multi-Company Ready

Settings are stored per company, supporting multi-company Odoo environments out of the box.
 
08

Seamless Integration

Works with the standard Odoo 16 Sales and Invoicing reports without custom themes.
How It Works
Three simple steps to enrich your documents with product images
 
1 Install the Module

Upload and install from the Apps menu on Odoo 16. No configuration wizard is required.
2 Enable in Settings

Go to Settings → Product Images and enable "Show Product Image in Reports". Optionally adjust the image width and height.
3 Print or Preview

Open a quotation, sale order, or customer invoice and print the standard PDF report.
