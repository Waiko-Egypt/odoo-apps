Module Overview
Enhance your business documents by automatically rendering each product's image inside Quotations, Sale Orders, and Customer Invoices.

With a single toggle in company settings, every report line shows a clear product photo next to the product name — making documents instantly recognisable to your customers and adding a professional touch to your printed output.
Key Features
 
 
01

Native Product Images

Uses each product's standard Odoo image (image_128) directly in printable business documents — no extra image upload needed.
 
02

Quotation & Sale Order PDF

Product images appear automatically in the standard sale quotation / order PDF report table, right next to the product description.
 
03

Invoice PDF Report

Product images are rendered inside customer invoice PDF reports, giving invoices a more visual and professional appearance.
 
04

Odoo 17 Standalone Settings

Dedicated settings page accessible from Settings → Product Images, with full Odoo 17 native layout and per-company support.
 
05

One-Click Enable / Disable

Toggle image display on or off from the general Settings page. When disabled, reports revert to their standard layout instantly.
 
06

Custom Image Dimensions

Control the exact width and height (in pixels) of product images in reports. Default is 40x40 px. Adjust to match your brand guidelines.
 
07

Multi-Company Support

Settings are stored per company on res.company, supporting multi-company Odoo environments out of the box.
 
08

Seamless Integration

Works with the standard Odoo 17 Sales and Invoicing reports. No custom themes or third-party modules required — zero conflicts.
How It Works
Three simple steps to enrich your documents with product images
 
1 Install the Module

Upload and install from the Apps menu on your Odoo 17 instance. No configuration wizard or extra setup is required.
2 Enable in Settings

Navigate to Settings → Product Images and enable the toggle "Show Product Image in Reports". Optionally adjust the image width and height (default: 40x40 px).
3 Print or Preview

Open any quotation, sale order, or customer invoice and print the standard PDF report. Product images will appear in a dedicated column next to each product name.
