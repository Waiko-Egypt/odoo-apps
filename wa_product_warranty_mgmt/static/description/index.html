Product Warranty Management

Configure product warranty periods and display warranty information on sales orders and customer invoices in Odoo 17.

Odoo 17.0

Main Features

Product Warranty Settings

Set a warranty duration in months or years from a dedicated Warranty tab on the product form.

Automatic Warranty Dates

Warranty start and end dates are calculated from the invoice date when a customer invoice or credit note is posted.

Sales Integration

Show the warranty description on sales order lines and on the printed quotation or sales order.

Invoice Integration

Show the warranty period and calculated dates on invoice lines and in the Odoo 17 invoice report.

How It Works

  1. Open a product and configure its warranty duration and period type.
  2. Add the product to a quotation or sales order.
  3. Create and post the customer invoice.
  4. Review the calculated warranty dates and print the invoice.

Compatibility

Designed for Odoo 17.0 Community and Enterprise editions.

Updated for the Odoo 17 product, sales order, invoice views, QWeb reports, and invoice posting workflow.

Required apps: Product, Sales Management, and Accounting.
